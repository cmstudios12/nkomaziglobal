GOOD TO KNOW

Admissions questions

Do I need an account to apply?

No. You can complete the online application without creating an account.

Will I receive a student number?

Yes, after successful submission, the system displays a unique application student number. Email delivery will be added later.

Does applying guarantee admission?

No. Your application must first be reviewed and accepted by the admissions team.

Can I apply from my phone?

Yes. This application has been designed for both mobile and desktop screens.
